Planning for peace of mind

Why prepare a funeral plan in advance?

Pre-need planning lets you record your preferences and review service options before your family has to make several decisions at once.

Clear preferences

Choose cremation or burial, a preferred funeral home, and the type of service you would like.

Less pressure on family

Your family can follow the decisions you documented instead of starting every detail from the beginning.

Time to review costs

You can review service items and payment choices carefully before an immediate need arises.

  • Arrange funds and services while the family is grieving.

Cremation

Cremation process

  1. Contact the designated funeral home after death is pronounced.
  2. The funeral home coordinates transportation, care, permits, and paperwork.
  3. The family may choose a viewing or memorial service.
  4. After required approvals, cremation takes place.
  5. The cremated remains are returned for interment, placement, or scattering as permitted.

Burial

Burial process

  1. Contact the designated funeral home for transportation and care.
  2. Care preparation and required permits are completed.
  3. The family may hold a viewing, funeral, or memorial service.
  4. The deceased is transported to the cemetery for the burial service.
  5. Review cemetery, vault, marker, and opening-and-closing items as applicable.

Timing, included services, and costs vary by funeral home, cemetery, crematory, location, and contract.

How the arrangement is structured

U.S. pre-need planning works differently from Korean mutual-aid plans

With a pre-need plan, the client discusses preferred services and costs in advance. Depending on the contract, funds may be managed through an insurance company or a trust structure rather than being held directly by the funeral home.

NGL Insurance Company was established in 1909 and has operated for more than a century. In an NGL insurance-based plan, funds are managed under the insurance contract; in a trust-based plan, funds are held and managed in trust.
